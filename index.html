How this dashboard works

Files and linking

  • Upload up to six CSV files. Each one is recognised by its columns and the separator is detected automatically: outlets (Identificativo), users (IDoperator), vouchers (Codice), recommendations (Sender account), nutrition plans (Proposal account) and customers (Registration date).
  • Vouchers are linked to outlets by ID only (Used in ID = outlet ID). Outlet names are never used for this, because several outlets share the same name.
  • A recommendation is tied to the outlet that created it by internal customer ID first, then by outlet name (using the category to choose between outlets with the same name). If it still can’t be tied, it is labelled by category only, for example Store (unlinked).

Outlet types

  • The outlets file lists every outlet. The type (Store, Vet, Breeder or Groomer) comes from its Category.
  • GC is a store that has a privacy acceptance date in the users file. Every other store is shown as Store, including stores that are missing from the users file.
  • Vets, breeders and groomers are never GC, even if they have a privacy acceptance date.
  • Test accounts (Managed by or Added by containing “test” or “SD Dev”) are hidden unless you tick Include test accounts.

Vouchers and NCs

  • Proposal vouchers are created by an NC. Prescription vouchers are created by a GC, vet, groomer or breeder. A small third type, voucher discount, is shown separately.
  • A voucher counts at the outlet where it was validated (Used in ID), on its validation date (Used at). It also carries the voucher amount. Vouchers whose outlet ID is not in the outlets file are left out.
  • The NC is the proposer on the voucher. Farmina Pet Foods and Farmina Genius AI count as NCs.
  • The Products tab uses the proposal and prescription voucher files. A purchase is a voucher that was used, with its product line, product, bag size and discount. A proposal voucher shows its product from the moment it is created, so created and used can be compared. A prescription voucher shows its product only once it is used. The table of plans and recommendations comes from the plans and recommendations exports. The date bar applies to the validation date for purchases and the creation date for created vouchers, plans and recommendations.
  • The proposal voucher file and the prescription voucher file list every voucher created, whether used or not. A single nutrition plan can create several voucher codes, so a plan is linked to all of its vouchers by its Proposal ID. A prescription voucher is linked to its recommendation by the second it was created (and the customer’s phone, when the voucher shows one).
  • When these files are loaded, a plan or recommendation counts as validated if any of its vouchers is used. Deleted vouchers are ignored. Without them, the export’s own status is used. The Amount column shows the amount used out of the amount issued. An unused prescription voucher shows the range of discounts it offers.
  • In the Outlets tab, the NC column beside an outlet shows which NCs’ proposals were validated there. It does not mean the NC is assigned to the outlet.

Who counts as one customer

  • A customer is identified by phone number (last 10 digits). An email is used only when it belongs to a single phone number, or when a record has no phone. An email that appears with two or more different phone numbers (for example a store entering its own email so it receives OTPs faster) is ignored for matching, so those customers stay separate.
  • Every nutrition plan and every recommendation or prescription is one event. Deleted plans and cancelled recommendations are ignored. Events with no usable phone or email can’t be matched to a person and are skipped.
  • A voucher is validated when a recommendation’s store voucher is used, or a nutrition plan is confirmed.

Unique, new, follow-up and returning

  • Unique (counted): a customer’s first-ever plan or recommendation that was created and validated (New), or a validated one made 12 or more months after their previous validated one (Re-counted). A plan or recommendation the customer never validated is not counted.
  • Follow-up: any later validated plan or recommendation within 12 months of the previous validated one. It is a return visit and adds nothing to unique customers. The 12 months are measured from the previous validated purchase.
  • Returning customer: a person who came again, meaning they have at least one follow-up or re-counted event.
  • The unique credit goes to whoever made that plan or recommendation, the NC or the outlet. It does not go to the outlet where the voucher was validated.
  • Validated events are ordered by exact creation date and time, so if one customer gets a recommendation and a plan on the same day, whoever created theirs first gets the unique credit.
  • Not validated: created, but the coupon was never validated. These are not counted as customers anywhere. In the NC summary, NP created = plans made, NP validated = plans whose coupon was validated, and NP validated minus Unique = follow-ups.
  • Your files cover only the dates you upload. Without earlier history, a customer marked New is new only within this data.

Dates and filters

  • The date bar filters vouchers by validation date, and recommendations and plans by creation date. Outlet counts are not affected, because the outlets file has no date.
  • Uniqueness is worked out from each customer’s full history first, and the dates are applied afterwards. A customer who first bought last year and buys again this month shows as a follow-up this month.
  • The dropdowns at the top of the Customers tab (source, NC, outlet, state, city, counted as) apply to the cards, charts and customer list on that tab. The tables by source, NC and top outlets always show created and validated side by side, and the NC summary has its own month picker. Customers are counted only if they validated; choose Not validated under Counted as to see the rest. State and city are the customer’s own for nutrition plans, and the creating outlet’s for recommendations.
  • Group by changes the time charts to day, week, month or year. The NC summary has its own month picker.
  • Look up many customers at once (Customers tab) takes a pasted list of phone numbers or emails, shows each match with its voucher counts, and saves an Excel report with every customer’s full history.
  • Clicking a customer’s name in the Customers list shows all their plans, recommendations and validated vouchers.
  • Clicking a row in the NC, source or top-outlets tables, a Unique, NP created or NP validated number in the NC summary, or the Unique customers number in the Outlets tab, lists the customers behind that number.

How data is cleaned

  • States are standardised by spelling, known short forms, close matches, or by using the state most outlets in the same city have. Anything left over shows as Unknown.
  • City names are merged and tidied, for example Bengaluru and Bangalore.
  • Coordinates with letters or degree format are converted. Missing or invalid ones are estimated from other outlets in the same city and drawn faintly on the map.
  • If an outlet has no displayed name, its company or contact name is shown instead.

Saving and privacy

  • A team copy of this dashboard opens with a password and is view only. It shows data that the owner exported. The data file is encrypted in the owner’s browser with that password before it is shared, and the password is never stored, so it cannot be recovered: if it is lost, export again with a new one.
  • Your files are kept in this browser only and reload when you reopen the page. They are not sent to any server.
  • Use the ✕ beside a saved file, or Delete all, to remove it. By default a new file is added to the saved data: records with the same ID are updated and older records are kept (matched by outlet ID, user ID, voucher code, plan or recommendation ID, or email and phone for customers). Choose Replace saved data next to the upload button to overwrite instead. Save backup downloads everything to a file you can keep, and Restore backup loads it again, so the data survives even if you clear your browser data or move to another computer.
  • Download Excel (on the Outlets tab and the Customers tab) saves a workbook of what you are currently looking at, with your filters and dates applied. It includes every row, not only the rows shown on screen. If you open the dashboard as a saved file on your computer, the file downloads through your browser as usual.
  • Voucher codes and amounts shown next to a plan or recommendation come from the voucher file, matched by the same customer and the same creation time. The voucher file lists validated vouchers only, so a plan that was never validated shows no code.
  • Clearing your browser data also removes the saved files, and they won’t appear on another device.
